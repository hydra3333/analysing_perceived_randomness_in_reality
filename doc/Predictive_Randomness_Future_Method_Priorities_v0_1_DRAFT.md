## 1. Purpose and context

Once the current system implementation is complete and the owner initiates further method development, reconsider the order of the outstanding experiments with Claude. Seek a shared recommendation where possible, retaining substantive differences for the owner's decision.

The project is an intentionally far-out investigation of perceived randomness in physical reality. Null results are expected and are valuable scientific outcomes. The question here is which experiments are most interesting, and perhaps most promising in a wildly optimistic sense, rather than which conventional statistical model would ordinarily be preferred.

The owner has stated that his current favourite is the de Broglie-related research. This is a preference for discussion, not a ratified change of sequence.

There are 62 catalogued E-families, of which 12 are implemented (E_1-E_4, E_6-E_10, E_17-E_19), with 40 released variants per game. The remaining 50 families are retained. OUTLANDISH_01 is separate completed research: the owner reports a null finding with very few draw-result matches across six constants of 10^10 digits each. It is closed and will not be integrated. It is not part of the outstanding method workload.

The existing roadmap remains in force pending the owner's decision. Do not interrupt build step 3 or step 4 to implement any of the proposals below.

## 3. Special consideration: de Broglie-Bohm-inspired research

Given the owner's current preference, consider bringing Wave 10 forward rather than leaving all six of its families until after the more conventional waves.

The existing catalogue already defines the following distinct hypotheses; no new identities are proposed here.

| Family | Distinct experimental idea |
|---|---|
| E_57 (C-PW1) | Fixed-size determinantal seven-set model: diversity/repulsion beyond the basic without-replacement constraint. A quantum/fermion-inspired mathematical analogy rather than a guided-trajectory model. |
| E_58 (C-PW2) | Unconditioned coupled latent-wave field: constrained complex amplitudes and norm-preserving propagation; fitted from history but no draw-by-draw state correction in the reference model. |
| E_59 (C-PW3) | Draw-conditioned coupled latent-wave field: a filtered state updated by past observations and propagated to the next target. Compare with E_58 to distinguish the effect of conditioning. |
| E_60 (C-PW4) | Post-change non-equilibrium relaxation analogue: investigate transient deviations and relaxation towards a reference propensity after a declared or historically inferred change. |
| E_61 (C-PW5A) | Guided-particle circulation: a common time-dependent complex field guides hidden labelled particles on an abstract ring, with selection near a capture region. Reference dynamics preserve cyclic ordering; observed draws supply evidence about the state rather than directly altering it. |
| E_62 (C-PW5B) | Guided-particle circulation with reinjection: extend E_61's framework so past selections explicitly change the latent particle state. Compare with E_61; reinjection may alter cyclic ordering. |

A possible practical sequence for discussion, distinct from the top-five curiosity ranking, is:

1. E_58 followed by E_59 (or a small jointly designed pair, retaining both identities). Establish a constrained, mathematically correct field and legal selection rule before adding the observation-conditioned update.
2. E_45 as a relatively accessible independent nonlinear approach, either before the field pair or shortly afterwards if the owner prefers a pilot-wave-first programme.
3. E_61 followed by E_62, using the simplest scientifically meaningful guided-trajectory reference and then adding the separately specified feedback/reinjection hypothesis.
4. E_48 when suitable time-available external data and its precise hypothesis are agreed; E_52 as a complementary latent-dynamics approach. The relative order may change with feasibility.
5. E_57 and E_60 remain valuable distinct hypotheses; consider bringing either forward if it offers a simpler useful experiment. Retain the rest of the catalogue and all remaining research leads.

This is an illustrative discussion sequence, not a full replacement wave schedule. E_58 and E_61 are natural mathematical foundations for E_59 and E_62 respectively; their earlier placement reflects practical reuse and comparison, not a higher estimated probability of success. Do not require every pilot-wave family to be completed before exploring other interesting methods. Avoid turning these relationships into unnecessary administrative gates.

The catalogue also preserves six unnumbered pilot-wave research leads: constrained free-beta fields; regularised wave-node motion; independently noisy positions; two-dimensional torus; cross-barrel coupling; and full configuration-space/entangled waves. Keep these available for later discussion and specification. The entangled-wave concept is particularly adventurous, but not yet sufficiently specified to assign a development priority.

## 4. Scientific and practical boundaries

- These are mathematical analogues and empirical forecasting experiments, not assertions that quantum pilot waves, Bohmian trajectories or physical entanglement govern lottery balls. An observed predictive association would not establish that physical mechanism.
- No listed method has demonstrated predictive advantage over independent fair draws in this project. Interest and implementation effort are subjective assessments; there is no defensible numerical success probability or evidence-based ranking yet. Complex models and sparse histories present substantial overfitting and identifiability risks.
- Preserve strict N-1 isolation, including fitting, filtering, initialization, external-data availability, seeds and model selection at each target. Distinguish retrospective/exploratory evidence from prevailing prospective claims. Keep the games separate; use legal selection rules and correct probability normalization where probability is claimed. Both TOP and WORST derive from the same frozen calculation.
- Specify variants and numerical methods before implementation. For initial versions use proportionate model capacity, ordinary existing numerical libraries where sufficient, and meaningful controls; do not change frozen identities or silently discard correlated or poor performers. Synthetic diagnostics inform interpretation and engineering, never replace real data or become an eligibility veto.
- Follow the agreed working principle: precise science, software methods, calculations and reporting; simple engineering wherever sufficient. Benchmark genuinely expensive inference before expanding it. Do not build additional general-purpose infrastructure merely to accommodate hypothetical future variants.
- Retain NL-means (reminder R-002), all other numbered and unnumbered ideas, and the deferred performance-engineering investigation. OUTLANDISH_01 remains closed.
