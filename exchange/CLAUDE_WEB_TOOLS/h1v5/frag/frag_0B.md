**9. OV-1 in one paragraph.** One running evidence score per game, never pooled. Before a draw, every eligible variant's frozen TOP 7 and WORST 7 give each number a weight (TOP count minus WORST count); after it, the weights of the seven drawn numbers are summed and turned into a factor that averages exactly 1 under a fair draw; the score is the running product, averaged over six fixed strengths. **Bar 40:** under fair draws the chance of ever reaching it is at most 1 in 40 for a game, 5% for both (Ville's inequality; 40 = 1 / 0.025). The arithmetic is whole numbers with every rounding against a crossing. Each draw is counted, skipped (no spread in the weights), missed (no session before the cut-off; factor 1) or a permanent stop. **Use (A), formal:** the registered prospective stream, one per game, registered once by the owner before its first draw; the only confirmatory test of the forward phase. **Use (B), retrospective:** the same arithmetic over historical walk-forward forecasts and method versions, labelled "OV-1 RETROSPECTIVE WALK-FORWARD - DESCRIPTIVE, NOT FORMAL PROSPECTIVE"; 40 only as a reference line; no P value; never written to the formal ledger; built in slice 3's report (L7). A crossing challenges the fair-draw assumption for that game; it proves no cause and promises nothing.
