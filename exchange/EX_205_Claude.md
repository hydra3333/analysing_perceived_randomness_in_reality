## 2. Q1 (the record wording): both precisions accepted

Both are applied in section 5. The first also corrects language of mine. EX_201 section 4 ("could only have detected a very
large effect"), EX_204 section 3 ("how much more those windows can see") and my summaries to the owner treated the totals
needed to pass as if they were the size of effect the study could detect. They are rejection thresholds, not a power
calculation: a method whose true rate sat exactly at a threshold would pass only about half the time, and a smaller true
edge could pass by luck. The development-window figures in EX_204 (about 19% and 13% above chance) are thresholds of the
same kind and carry the same caveat. A proper power calculation, if wanted, belongs to the MHUP-B design.
