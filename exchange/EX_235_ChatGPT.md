## 1. Why 40 is retained and what it does - NOT mystical or a discovery threshold

Ville's inequality for a valid nonnegative supermartingale E_n with E_0 <= 1 gives

  P_fair( sup_{n>=0} E_n >= B ) <= 1 / B.

The chosen programme-level conventional false-alarm allowance is 5%; split as 2.5% per game for Powerball and Oz Lotto (not pooled). Therefore B = 1 / 0.025 = 40 for EACH independently maintained game stream. By the union bound the chance of either crossing is <= 5% under the relevant joint fair-draw assumptions; independence between games is not required to add the bounds. The 5% allocation is a design choice, not a physical constant, and 40 is the mathematically corresponding threshold, not an arbitrary 'lucky' number.

Keep formal threshold 40, its six registered lambdas (-.20, -.10, -.05, +.05, +.10, +.20) and equal mixture, exact integer fair-draw distribution, certified noninflated factors, immutable running record, and the registered 2.5%-per-game inference. A threshold crossing is a challenge to the specified fair-draw null, NOT proof of causation, advance knowledge, a physical model, or future predictive power.

A retrospective display may show 40 as an explicitly DESCRIPTIVE REFERENCE line because the arithmetic is comparable. But DO NOT attach '2.5% chance', 'p < .025', 'significant under registered OV-1', or an anytime-valid false-alarm assertion to a retrospective crossing when the historical selection procedure was not covered by the prospective assumptions. The numerical value 40 in a chart does not transfer the null error guarantee.
