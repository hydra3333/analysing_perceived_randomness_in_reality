R-1 - FIXED 01:30 UTC CUT-OFF: ACCEPT.

Independent timezone check agrees with cutoff_check.py:
on all 1,996 Tuesday/Thursday dates 2026-11-17 to
2045-12-31, 01:30 UTC is 12:00 Adelaide in daylight
time (1,002 dates), or 11:00 Adelaide in standard
time (994 dates). It is NEVER later than the owner's
noon and is eight hours before 19:30 AEST = 09:30

conservative operational choice, not an equivalent

R-2 - MISSED RULE AND STOP ARGUMENT: MATH PASS;
EVIDENCE QUALIFICATION REQUIRED (RC-225-1).

Under the conditional fair-draw null, if the choice
between an eligible pre-cut-off frozen table and
factor 1 is a function of the history KNOWN BY THE
CUT-OFF, it is predictable. The exact certified
factor has conditional mean <=1 and the missed
factor is 1, so the registered mixture remains a
nonnegative supermartingale. If a STOP freezes its
last reported value and no later factor/crossing
can be claimed, reported crossings are a SUBSET
of those in the valid unstopped stream. Thus
even an after-outcome stop cannot manufacture
a crossing. This subset argument is correct.
It does NOT rescue an outcome-dependent choice
of whether a draw was "missed".

What needs correction: original installed design
v0.1r7 section 6.5 expressly states that a
digest-chain check proves internal agreement but
does NOT, by itself, prove WHEN lines were written
or that the whole chain was never replaced.
Sup v0.8 6.8 and plan 6.6 sometimes speak as
though a verified chain alone establishes the
COMPLETE pre-cut-off absence of a freeze line.
A valid-looking truncated/replaced prefix could
still pass an internal chain check; a local clock
can be wrong/backdated. Later absence must NOT be
advertised as externally proved by a digest alone.

RC-225-1: Make the conditional operational
assumption explicit, in Sup 6.8 and plan 6.6:
the rule is valid ONLY with faithful append-only
writer operation, trustworthy local event order
and clock relative to the actual pre-outcome
cut-off, and no retrospective suppression or
replacement of an eligible freeze line.
The source record plus verified chain is
LOCAL operational evidence, NOT independent
proof of absence or publication time. Where
an earlier trusted chain head/witness or
session/ticket record exists, reconcile it;
a contradiction or uncertain chain BLOCKS
and must not be turned into MISSED. Include
tests for missing/torn and conflicting
prefixes, backward clock and any available

weaker-witness label already proposed for

not a demand for an external witness or
new owner step every missed week.
